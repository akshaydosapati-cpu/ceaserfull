Built on enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure.

CEASER is designed with security, privacy, and reliability at its core. Authentication, storage, databases, and AI services are powered by trusted technology partners with industry-standard security practices.

Encrypted Communication

Communication between your device and CEASER is protected using HTTPS/TLS.

AUTH

Account access is handled using modern authentication standards and user-scoped sessions.
